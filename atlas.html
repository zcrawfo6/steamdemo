About this data. Computed live from internship_records.csv by internship_atlas_server.py: for each state, the most common internship field, then the most common role and employer within that field, plus the undergraduate / graduate split. Edit the CSV and refresh to update the map. Logos render for employers covered by the open-source Simple Icons set; others show a ticker-style mark.
